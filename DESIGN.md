# Teska Baje editor

Brief: one local photo or video, simple framing, draggable caption, built-in audio, download/share. Sharp corners and neutral colors take precedence over decorative styling.

Palette: paper #ffffff, workspace #f3f3f1, stage #e9e9e6, ink #242523, muted #6a6c66, border #d9dad4. Black filled buttons identify primary actions. Colored swatches affect only the exported caption.

Type: Arimo sans for the tool. Arimo, Libre Baskerville, Oswald and Kalam caption fonts for captions, with Nepali font fallback. Sentence case labels and tabular numerals for time.

Layout: compact top bar, task heading, broad preview on the left and bounded editing controls on the right. Original-size aspect selection above the canvas. Playback below. A single export row anchors the control panel. On mobile the preview comes first and compact tabs keep tools within reach.
